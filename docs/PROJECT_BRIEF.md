## The story (new concept, locked)

**Barry and his buddy are faking Bigfoot.** The buddy wears a cheap brown Bigfoot costume on short stilts. Barry is the "witness" photographer. A blurry photo is the goal, because a clear photo would show the zipper. Every blobsquatch photo means the hoax worked.

- **Success, for them:** the photo comes out blurry and people believe it.
- **Failure, for them:** someone gets close enough to see the zipper and the eyes. The visitor is that someone ("You had to be NEAR").

| Moment | What happens | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Walking away (far) | He looks like a real Bigfoot. Up close you see the zipper with its NEAR-green pull. | The setup |
| The snap | Barry steps on a twig on purpose, as the cue. The guy does the rehearsed look-back. The camera flash lights him up, and his real human eyes show through the mask's eye holes. | The near-miss you get to see |
| The photo develops | A brown blur | The hoax worked |
| Chart pumping | Barry and the guy dance. The loose mask bounces and shows his chin and face. On big pumps it can pop off. | People are believing it |
| Chart dumping | The guy freezes holding his mask on, and Barry hides the camera. Or Barry dozes, as on the current site. | "They're onto us" |
| Big milestone | The mask comes off in a behind-the-scenes photo of Barry and the guy high-fiving. The guy puts his glasses back on. | The full reveal |
| "Get in the Suit" | Pay in $BLOBSQUATCH to have your own face in the suit for a day. | You join the hoax |

**NEARsighted:** the guy had to take his glasses off to wear the mask, so he can't see well. That's why he's clumsy and why the glasses trait is rare.

**Why he's agile and elusive** (NEAR is fast): he can't let anyone get close, or they'll see the zipper. That's also the game's mechanic.

**Why every photo is blurry:** a clear photo would expose the costume.

## The "blobsquatch" term (research)

- **Origin:** coined on the Bigfoot Forums around 2002 by Vito Quaranta and spread by Ray Randell in 2002–2003. The first public lecture use was September 14, 2003, by Alton Higgins at the Willow Creek Symposium (source: cryptomundo.com, "The Short History of Blobsquatch").
- **No single "first" photo.** It was later applied to older photos, such as the 1986 Woolridge "Yeti" photo.
- **Definition (Urban Dictionary, 2008):** "a vague, dark blob in photos or video presented as Bigfoot — could be a person in a gorilla suit, a bear, or a smudge on the lens." The costume idea is built into the word.
- **The 1967 Patterson-Gimlin film** (descriptions range from "dark reddish-brown" and "silvery brown" to "black"):
  - Bob Heironimus claimed he wore the suit, made from a red horse's hide.
  - Costume maker Philip Morris claimed he sold a brown Dynel gorilla suit.
  - Don't use frames from the film. The footage is someone else's property.
