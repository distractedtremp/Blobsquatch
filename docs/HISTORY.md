# How we got here (read before proposing "new" ideas)

## Name

The name went RABES → $RABIES → $GUANO (dropped) → **Blobsquatch / $BLOBSQUATCH**. The original NEAR idea was near/far, and that's still the core joke: **you had to be NEAR.**

## Character evolution

1. **A pink code-drawn blob (site v1–v14).** Simple ellipses with a blank "blah" face. The owner later said this deadpan blankness on the pump screen was **funnier** than what replaced it.
2. **"Flat Fuzz" pink sprite (v15, the live site).** Cut from an AI master (pink fur, rose face/hands/feet, lighter belly) with sassy/happy faces. **Sassy was rejected** as less funny.
3. **Clueless pink Bigfoot explorations.**
   - Color tests: purple, pink+white, NEAR-green skin. Green skin looked like a goblin or witch, and the owner rejected renaming to "BlobGoblin".
   - A three-color maximum rule.
   - The owner's "NEAR reveal" idea: pink from far away, NEAR-green skin when you get close. **Superseded.**
4. **Face problems.**
   - A pig nose, then a face that resembled Sandy Cheeks (SpongeBob). That's an IP risk, so it was avoided.
   - Mouths: hinge line, tiny O, side squiggle.
   - A "shifty side-glance" was proposed and **rejected**. He doesn't want personality traits to set Blobsquatch apart.
5. **Reimagined as a normal sasquatch** with only two unique traits, NEARsighted and agile/elusive. The lean pink sasquatch came out skinny and weak, and the teen version came out as a chimp. **Rejected.**
6. **The breakthrough (owner's idea): he's a guy in a Bigfoot costume.** From far away he's Bigfoot; up close it's a costume.
   - **Pink dropped** in favor of the 1967 reddish/dark brown.
   - **The green zipper pull** replaced a green T-shirt, because a hoaxer wouldn't wear a non-camo shirt, but a cheap zipper can be any color.
   - **The hood failed** in three tries: flimsy, read as a pet monkey, read as a hat. It became a **separate mask held like an astronaut's helmet.**
   - **The semi-realistic painted version "nailed it"** as a concept, but the man looked too realistic. That led to the **MS Paint degen style** (Virgin-vs-Chad energy).
   - **Gemini's "degen" try was rejected.** It was disheveled and full of crypto clichés (Pepe, Bored Ape, Monster cans, laser eyes), and it used other people's IP.
   - **Proportion passes:**
     - The head went from too small (1/12) to too big to about 1/8.
     - Hidden stilts were added (owner's idea).
     - The shoulders went from a skinny tube to fridge humps to sloped Bigfoot shoulders.
     - The mask changed from gorilla to Bigfoot.
     - The forehead mark went from a red cut to a soft pink line.
7. **Barry joins the hoax** (owner's idea). That untangled "success vs failure": a blurry photo means success.

## Rejected or parked ideas, and why

- **Paying to put a famous person in the suit.** Right-of-publicity and AI-likeness laws. It was replaced by "Get in the Suit," using your own face.
- **Fundraising for "Blobsquatch's glasses" or a naming goal.** It looks like a use-of-proceeds promise (securities risk), and a new name would split the brand. He stays NEARsighted; glasses are a rare trait or part of the reveal.
- **Cute and cuddly / plush-first, Sully-like.** The owner wants a plush eventually, but it must not copy Sully (Monsters, Inc.). No horns, spots or teal.
- **Earlier art styles rejected:** a real felt puppet, a 1967-era cartoon, Jetsons/space-age mockups ("ok, not premium"), and a 3D designer toy.
- **Copying the Chad drawing, Pepe, Pink Panther, Labubu and similar characters.** IP.
- **The "Blobsquash" Halloween variant,** squash-colored. Parked as a seasonal idea.
- **Making the site's green the NEAR logo or putting the "N" on him.** Not allowed. It's NEAR's trademark, and the footer says the project isn't affiliated.
