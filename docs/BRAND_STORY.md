# Brand story: NEAR mascot first, GTA as the wink

Decided 2026-10-26 after the owner asked whether to go "GTA narrative" or "NEAR mascot." Answer: both, in different jobs. The identity is the NEAR mascot. The GTA bit is the launch campaign. They are not in conflict, because one answers "who is he" and the other answers "why now."

## The split

| | NEAR mascot | GTA 6 campaign |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Identity. Who he is, forever. | Timing. Why this month. |
| Lives in | Ticker, logo, bio, headline, tagline, token description | One origin paragraph on the site, the sighting petition, launch-week posts |
| Lifespan | Years | Three weeks. It ends on November 19, 2026, GTA 6 release day. |
| Ownable | Yes. Bigfoot is folklore, NEAR green is our color, Barry is ours. | No. It is Rockstar's game. We can only wink at it. |
| Crowd | NEAR holders, who have no mascot coin. Empty lane. | GTA fans, who are mostly on Solana buying GATO-type coins. Crowded lane. |

The joke that ties them: Bigfoot is the thing everybody says doesn't exist. So is a NEAR meme coin. "You had to be NEAR."

## Do not do these

1. Do not rename, re-ticker or re-logo around GTA. It expires on November 19 and it is someone else's trademark.
2. Do not become "a GTA coin on NEAR." The GTA crowd is on Solana; we would be the eleventh GTA coin instead of the first NEAR mascot.
3. Do not run two headlines. One headline (NEAR mascot), one wink (GTA origin). Never both in the same post.
4. Do not delay launch for art polish. Memecoins win on timing and community. Missing the pre-November-19 window costs more than an imperfect pose.
5. Do not promise Rockstar anything. The petition is a joke. Never "in talks," never "partnership," never Rockstar logos, fonts, characters or screenshots.

## The sighting petition (how it works without a backend)

The site is static, so it cannot store signatures. The petition is an X post. "Sign the sighting log" opens a pre-filled post: "I have seen Blobsquatch. #BlobsquatchForGTA6 @BlobsquatchNEAR" with a blurry sticker attached. The site shows a tally (hand-updated from the hashtag count, or pulled from Telegram member count) and links to the hashtag search so people see the pile. Half a day of work, no wallet, no server.
